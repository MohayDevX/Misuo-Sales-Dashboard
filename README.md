# Misuo Sales & Operations Dashboard



## Business Overview

Managing sales, tracking product preferences, and monitoring delivery performance across different regions and platforms is essential for e-commerce growth. This project features an advanced Excel-based dashboard designed for "MISUO" to provide a clear, real-time analytical view of sales performance, customer distribution, and operational fulfillment.



## Problem Statement

Retail and manufacturing operations often struggle with fragmented sales data. This dashboard addresses key business metrics by solving:
1. *Revenue & Order Tracking:* Centralizing high-level KPIs including Total Sales (1M+), Total Orders, and Average Selling prices.
2. *Platform & Regional Performance:* Identifying top-performing sales platforms and geographical areas (governorates) to optimize marketing and logistics.
3. *Product Trend Analysis:* Tracking best-selling items and color preferences to align inventory planning with actual customer demand.
4. *Fulfillment & Delivery Monitoring:* Evaluating delivery status breakdowns and on-time delivery rates to ensure high service quality and customer satisfaction.


## Key Dashboard Features

Built entirely using *Microsoft Excel (Pivot Tables & Pivot Charts)*, the dashboard includes dynamic filtering and interactive visuals:
* *KPI Summary Cards:* Instant visibility into Total Sales, Average Selling, Total Orders, and Best Seller items.
* *Platform & Area Breakdown:* Visual bar charts detailing sales distribution across various e-commerce platforms and geographic locations.
* *Customer Segmentation:* Comparative analysis between individual buyers and corporate clients (Individuals vs. Companies).
* *Delivery Status & Slicers:* Interactive Slicers on the side for seamless filtering by platform and governorate, paired with donut charts tracking on-time delivery efficiency (82%).


## Tools & Technologies

* *Microsoft Excel:* Data organization, cleaning, and structuring.
* *Pivot Tables & Pivot Charts:* Aggregating data and building dynamic visual components.
* *Excel Slicers:* Interactive filtering for a user-friendly analytical experience.
